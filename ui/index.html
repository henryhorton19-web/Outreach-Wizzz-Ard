How to use Paris Outreach

This app writes first-draft outreach emails to companies and funds you're targeting for a part-time seat during your Sciences Po year, so you start from a solid draft instead of a blank page. You review and edit every draft; nothing is ever sent automatically. Approving stages the email as a file you open in your mail client and records the send in your tracker.

Voices you control

The engine reads each target's situation and auto-matches a voice: no role at a small company (a create-the-seat pitch), a role at a small company, or a role at a large company. Open Voices in the header to edit any voice, create your own, or delete the ones that ship — nothing is fixed. Per block you choose your exact words or let the model write from your guidance. Set a default voice, force one for a whole session, or switch a single draft from its drawer.

Creating drafts

Paste target names (one per line) or upload a list or your Outreach_Tracker workbook, then click Draft. The app researches each target for two proof points and one recent trigger, ties them to your background, and writes a first draft. Drafts save automatically.

Reviewing and editing

Click any draft to open it. On the left you see the research and the parts of your profile the engine tied in, with source links. On the right is the full email; the greeting, opening and ask are locked as the house frame, and the body is yours to edit. Your changes save as you go.

The "contact unverified" note

If a draft shows "contact unverified," the app couldn't fully confirm the recipient. It still fills in a best guess; double-check before sending. A target may also be marked disqualified if the role needs presence outside Paris or is French-dominant.

Approving and sending

When a draft is ready, approve it. The app stages a .eml file you can open in your mail client and writes the send to your tracker's "Reach Out To" sheet. Nothing leaves your outbox until you press send yourself.

Voices

Every voice is editable data, including the three that ship. Each target is auto-matched to a voice by its situation; you can override for this session or per draft.

These voices write the follow-up emails in the Follow-ups tab. Same editor, separate set. Defaults follow best practice: short, one new angle, a single ask, same subject thread.
